VGMSTREAM - the game's sounds

Put here:   vgmstream-cli.exe
            and the .dll files that come with it. Keep them together in this folder:
            vgmstream-cli.exe needs its .dll files next to it.
            Easiest: unzip the whole vgmstream-win64.zip into this folder.

Download:   https://vgmstream.org  (the "vgmstream-win64.zip" build; tested with r2117)

When it is right, this file exists:
            TownfallCompanion\tools\vgmstream\vgmstream-cli.exe

Without it the phone has no sound.
