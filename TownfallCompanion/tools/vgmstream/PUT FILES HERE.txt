VGMSTREAM - the game's sounds

PUT THESE 12 FILES IN THIS FOLDER (all of them, together):

    vgmstream-cli.exe
    avcodec-vgmstream-59.dll
    avformat-vgmstream-59.dll
    avutil-vgmstream-57.dll
    libatrac9.dll
    libcelt-0061.dll
    libcelt-0110.dll
    libg719_decode.dll
    libmpg123-0.dll
    libspeex-1.dll
    libvorbis.dll
    swresample-vgmstream-4.dll

Where to get them: https://vgmstream.org - download "vgmstream-win64.zip" (tested with r2117)
and unzip it into this folder. All 12 files are in that zip.

Not needed (leave them or delete them): COPYING, README.md, USAGE.md and the .zip itself.
A newer vgmstream may name the .dll files differently: then keep every .dll file of its zip
together with vgmstream-cli.exe.

It is right when this file exists:
    ...\ue4ss\Mods\TownfallCompanion\tools\vgmstream\vgmstream-cli.exe

Without these files the phone has no sound.
