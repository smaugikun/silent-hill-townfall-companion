TOWNFALL COMPANION - TOOLS
==========================

Townfall Companion uses three free programs that are not part of this mod. Download each one
and put it in its own folder here:

  vgmstream\   puts the game's sounds on the phone
               put here:  vgmstream-cli.exe  and the .dll files that come with it

  rad\         reads the game's videos (Bink 2)
               put here:  radvideo64.exe  (unpack all of RADTools.7z)

  ffmpeg\      makes those videos small enough for the phone
               put here:  ffmpeg.exe

Open each folder: its "PUT FILES HERE.txt" says where to download the program.

The companion finds the programs by itself, anywhere below this folder (or on the PATH), so
there is nothing to set up. When it starts, its window names anything it can't find.
Without vgmstream the phone is silent. Without rad and ffmpeg the phone shows no game videos.

What you put here stays when you update the mod.
