TOWNFALL COMPANION - TOOLS FOLDER
=================================

Townfall Companion needs three free programs that are NOT part of this mod. Download each one
and put exactly these files in its folder here. Every folder also has its own "PUT FILES HERE.txt"
with the download link.

  tools\vgmstream\   (12 files)   the game's sounds
      vgmstream-cli.exe
      avcodec-vgmstream-59.dll
      avformat-vgmstream-59.dll
      avutil-vgmstream-57.dll
      libatrac9.dll
      libcelt-0061.dll
      libcelt-0110.dll
      libg719_decode.dll
      libmpg123-0.dll
      libspeex-1.dll
      libvorbis.dll
      swresample-vgmstream-4.dll

  tools\radtools\    (1 file)     the game's videos (Bink 2)
      radvideo64.exe

  tools\ffmpeg\      (1 file)     makes those videos small enough for the phone
      ffmpeg.exe

Other files that come with the downloads (README, COPYING, binkplay.exe, ffprobe.exe, the .zip) are not
used: leave them or delete them.

This "tools" folder must be next to the "companion" and "Scripts" folders:
  ...\ue4ss\Mods\TownfallCompanion\tools

The companion finds the files by itself, anywhere below this folder (or on the PATH), so there is
nothing to set up. When it starts, its window names anything it can't find.
Without vgmstream the phone is silent. Without radtools and ffmpeg the phone shows no game videos.

What you put here stays when you update the mod.
