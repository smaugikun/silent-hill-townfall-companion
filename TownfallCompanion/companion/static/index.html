Settings

VIEW: the CRTV is on the phone. Turn the phone to look around; ⌖ lines it up with your character. AV OUT: the CRTV is on the PC, with its picture and sound; the phone's screen stays dark. In both, the TUNING buttons and the D-pad work on the game's CRTV.

Device

In VIEW the game's CRTV is on either way, so its voices, subtitles and fine tuning come to the phone. On: your character raises it, as with the radio button on your keyboard or controller, and you see it on the monitor. Off: it works without showing on the monitor. Switching it takes effect at once.

Fine tuning raises the CRTV in both hands. Off: during it, the monitor doesn't show the CRTV or the hands holding it, however it was raised. Switching it takes effect at once.

In VIEW, with the phone in your hand: turn or tilt it and your character turns or looks up and down the same way. Not while it lies flat or stands still, or during fine tuning.

In VIEW, in the image stages of fine tuning, leaning and tilting the phone moves the picture as the mouse or the controller does. Higher moves it further for the same turn.

Picking the phone up, from the table or a stand, switches to VIEW; laying it flat to AV OUT. Not during fine tuning.

Set on a stand

With Auto pickup: what the phone switches to once it stands still on a stand.

Picture and sound

The CRTV's sound and the voices play on the phone.

What the phone plays, the PC doesn't.

Only the phone's own sound. At 0 the phone is silent and the game plays its sound again.

Vibration

A short buzz when you press a button or the dial clicks.

Pulses while tuned to a monster, stronger the closer it is; a buzz when a new signal appears.

Intensity

Makes each buzz longer or shorter.
