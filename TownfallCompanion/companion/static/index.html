Settings

VIEW: the CRTV is on the phone. Turn the phone to look around; ⌖ lines it up with your character. AV OUT: the CRTV is on the PC, with all its sound; the phone shows its picture or stays dark. In both, the TUNING buttons and F work on the game's CRTV.

Device

Your character raises the CRTV, as with L1 on a controller: voices, subtitles and the fine-tune mini-game work, and you see it on the monitor too. Off: only when you raise it in the game.

In VIEW, with the phone in your hand: turn it and your character turns the same way (tilting only moves the CRTV's own view). Not while it lies flat or stands still.

Picking the phone up, from the table or a stand, switches to VIEW; laying it flat to AV OUT.

Set on a stand

With Auto pickup: what the phone switches to once it stands still on a stand.
